About Us

At VSTravel, we believe travel is more than just reaching a destination—it's about the experiences, stories, and memories you create along the way. Whether you're chasing sunsets on tropical beaches, exploring historic cities, or planning a once-in-a-lifetime adventure, we're here to make it seamless. With a passion for discovery and a commitment to personalized service, VSTravel turns your travel dreams into unforgettable journeys.

Backed by a team of experienced travel experts and a global network of partners, we offer curated itineraries, flexible bookings, and 24/7 support—so you can travel with confidence and peace of mind. No matter where you're headed, we’re with you every step of the way.

History

Founded with a vision for redefining modern travel, VSTravel was established on May 1st, 2025, by Jason Kenneth Abraham in Indonesia. Inspired by the beauty of the islands, he set out to create a travel agency that blends personalized service with global reach. After weeks of development, planning, and passion-fueled work, VSTravel officially launched on May 24th, 2025—marking the beginning of a journey dedicated to helping others discover the world with ease and inspiration.
